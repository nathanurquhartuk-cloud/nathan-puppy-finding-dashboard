✅ Safe-Buying Checklist (avoid scams & puppy farms)

  • Always see the puppy with its mum at the breeder's home (Lucy's Law: buy direct from breeder or rescue only).
  • Verify KC registration yourself on The Kennel Club website before paying — ask for the dam's registered name. "DWKC" / "WDKC" / NEBBR are NOT The Kennel Club.
  • Ask for Bulldog Breed Council health scheme certificates (Bronze/Silver/Gold) and BOAS grades (0–1 is best).
  • Be wary of "rare colours" (lilac, merle, chocolate, blue) at premium prices — these are non-breed-standard and often bred for fashion, not health.
  • Never pay a full amount by bank transfer before visiting; typical deposit is ~£500 with a receipt/contract. Adverts far below market (e.g. KC pups at £1,200) are a scam red flag.
  • Check the breeder's council licence if they breed 3+ litters/year; Champdogs and Pets4Homes "ID verified" helps but is not proof.
  • Expect: vet check, microchip, first vaccination, worming, puppy pack and contract at handover.
  • Budget beyond purchase: insurance £60–£120+/month for bulldogs, plus potential BOAS surgery costs.

💷 Price Guide (verified 18 Sep 2026)

  • KC-registered, health-tested standard-colour litters: £2,300–£3,500 (most cluster at £2,500–£3,000).
  • Top show / Champion-sired lines: £3,500–£4,600 (Laroyal, Chayo, Bullmont lines; Champdogs kennels often "price on enquiry").
  • Non-standard colours (lilac, merle, blue tri): anywhere from £1,500 (end-of-litter discounts) to £4,000–£6,000 "pick of litter".
  • Old English Bulldog (NEBBR) types: £1,250–£2,000. Miniature (Bulldog x Frenchie) crosses: ~£1,800 when they appear.
  • Rescue adoption: nominal fee up to ~£400 (includes neutering/vaccination at general rescues).
