✅ Safe-Buying Checklist (avoid scams & puppy farms)

  • Always see the puppy with its mum at the breeder's home (Lucy's Law: buy direct from breeder or rescue only).
  • Verify KC registration yourself on The Kennel Club website before paying — ask for the dam's registered name.
  • Ask for Bulldog Breed Council health scheme certificates (Bronze/Silver/Gold) and BOAS grades (0–1 is best).
  • Be wary of "rare colours" (lilac, merle, chocolate) at premium prices — these are non-breed-standard and often bred for fashion, not health.
  • Never pay a full amount by bank transfer before visiting; typical deposit is ~£500 with a receipt/contract.
  • Check the breeder's council licence if they breed 3+ litters/year; Champdogs and Pets4Homes "ID verified" helps but is not proof.
  • Expect: vet check, microchip, first vaccination, worming, puppy pack and contract at handover.
  • Budget beyond purchase: insurance £60–£120+/month for bulldogs, plus potential BOAS surgery costs.

💷 Price Guide (verified Aug 2026)

  • KC-registered, health-tested litters from established/show kennels: £2,500–£3,500.
  • Top show/Crufts lines: up to £4,600 (e.g. Laroyal, East Sussex).
  • Non-KC / Old English Bulldog types: £1,450–£2,000.
  • Late-summer markdowns are real: older pups dropped to £1,800–£2,500 in Aug (Jedburgh, Mansfield, Washington).
  • Miniature (Bulldog x Frenchie) crosses: ~£900–£1,500 when they appear.
  • Rescue adoption: nominal fee up to ~£450 (Geordie Bullies £450; general rescues include neutering/vaccination).
