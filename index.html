✅ Safe-Buying Checklist (avoid scams & puppy farms)

  • Always see the puppy with its mum at the breeder's home (Lucy's Law: buy direct from breeder or rescue only).
  • Verify KC registration yourself on The Kennel Club website before paying — ask for the dam's registered name.
  • Ask for Bulldog Breed Council health scheme certificates (Bronze/Silver/Gold) and BOAS grades (0–1 is best).
  • Be wary of "rare colours" (lilac, merle, chocolate, blue) at premium prices — these are non-breed-standard and often bred for fashion, not health. Merle in particular is not a Bulldog colour.
  • Watch for non-KC registries used to look official — DWKC, NBS and NEBBR are not The Kennel Club.
  • Never pay a full amount by bank transfer before visiting; typical deposit is ~£500 with a receipt/contract.
  • Deposits on unborn litters are the main December route — insist on a written refund policy if the litter is smaller than scanned or you are not matched.
  • Check the breeder's council licence if they breed 3+ litters/year; Champdogs and Pets4Homes "ID verified" helps but is not proof.
  • Expect: vet check, microchip, first vaccination, worming, puppy pack and contract at handover.
  • Budget beyond purchase: insurance £60–£120+/month for bulldogs, plus potential BOAS surgery costs.

💷 Price Guide (verified 4 Sep 2026)

  • KC-registered, health-tested litters from established/show kennels: £2,500–£3,800.
  • Top show/Crufts lines: £4,000–£4,600 (Laroyal, East Sussex; Snorabulls, York; Deluxabulls, Manchester).
  • Non-KC / Old English Bulldog types: £1,400–£2,000.
  • Miniature (Bulldog x Frenchie) crosses: ~£900–£1,800 when they appear.
  • Rescue adoption: nominal fee up to ~£400 (includes neutering/vaccination at general rescues).
  • Trend since August: several summer litters have cut prices hard to clear the last pup — Jedburgh £3,000→£1,800, Mansfield £2,750→£2,000, Wigan £2,600→£2,500. Late, unsold puppies are where the discounts are.
